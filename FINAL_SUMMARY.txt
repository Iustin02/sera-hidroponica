📊 DISEASE CLASSES (5 Total)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

0: Healthy (Sănătos) 🌿
   Basil plant in perfect health, no visible diseases

1: Downy Mildew (Mucegai pufos) 🌫️
   White powdery coating on leaf undersides

2: Bacterial Leaf Spot (Pată bacteriană) 🔴
   Brown/black spots with water-soaked appearance

3: Leaf Miner Damage (Daunele mineri) 🪲
   Tunneling patterns and winding trails in leaves

4: General Disease (Alte boli) ⚠️
   Other basil diseases and health issues

🚀 HOW TO USE (3 SIMPLE STEPS)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: DOWNLOAD DATASET
────────────────────────
1. Open in browser:
   https://universe.roboflow.com/disease-detection-2r3sj/basil-health-detection-9crhs

2. Click "Download" button (green, top right)

3. Select "YOLOv8" format

4. Click "Download" to save ZIP to ~/Downloads/

STEP 2: RUN AUTOMATION PIPELINE
───────────────────────────────
cd /Users/iustiniuliandodita/sera-hidroponica/
python3 full_pipeline.py

The script will:
  • Wait for your ZIP download to complete
  • Extract the ZIP automatically
  • Import 1,670 images to basil_dataset/
  • Verify dataset integrity
  • Train YOLOv8 model (50 epochs)
  • Save best model to runs/detect/train/weights/best.pt

STEP 3: USE YOUR MODEL
──────────────────────
# View results
open runs/detect/train/

# Use for predictions
python3 inference.py --source basil_dataset/images/val/

# Or in Python
from ultralytics import YOLO
model = YOLO("runs/detect/train/weights/best.pt")
results = model.predict(source="path/to/basil/images/")
